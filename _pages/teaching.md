Some of my teaching activities while a PhD candidate @ Cambridge involve over 60 hours of demonstrations for the Engineering Undergraduate Tripos within the broad field of aerospace engineering:\

* **Engineering Tripos Part IIA Project, SA1: Aircraft Wing Analysis (23/24) :** Guiding students through building an end-to-end MATLAB software suite for aerodynamics calculations using the panel method.\

* **Engineering Tripos Part IB Experiments: T4 Heat Transfer (24/25) :** Demonstrations in convective heat transfer using a Wheatstone bridge circuit. Demonstrations in Schlieren photography.

* **Engineering Tripos Part IIA, 3A1: Fluid Mechanics I: Turbulent Boundary Layers (24/25) :** Guiding students through wind tunnel experiments to observe laminar-turbulent flow transitions via hot-wire anemometry and a stethoscope.\

Other teaching activities:

* **FLOW Summer School, KTH Royal Institute of Technology (Stockholm, Sweden) :** Practicals in image processing of high-speed fluorescence data (~20 kHz).
